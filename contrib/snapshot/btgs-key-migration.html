Before you start

Your private keys are your coins. Anyone who sees them can take them. No one from the BTGS team will ever ask you for your keys, your wallet file, your seed words or the files this page creates.

Disconnect from the internet (Wi-Fi off or cable out) before you load your keys. This page works offline. Do not take screenshots of the keys or post the files anywhere.

4. Import into the new wallet

Download or copy the import line. In the NEW BTGS Core wallet open Window > Console, type importdescriptors, a space, a single quote ', paste the line, another single quote ', and press Enter. The single quotes are needed, the console does not accept the line without them. The wallet then scans the chain by itself.
